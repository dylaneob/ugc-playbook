# Trybe beyond Nate West: public-source research

Researched 2026-10-06. Nate West (@watedontwait) excluded. Labels: [CLAIM] = creator earnings, or vendor, agency or competitor marketing, unaudited. VERIFIED = I loaded the page and saw it. UNVERIFIED = snippet, summary or nothing. No source found = I searched and found nothing.

**Bottom line.** The public record on Trybe is thin and mostly written by people who profit from it (agencies, competitors, a course seller). The one hard primary document is the creator ToS. The sharpest creator risk: an approved video earns nothing until a brand runs it as an ad.

**Coverage limits.** The search tool cannot reach Reddit and the fetch tool blocked old.reddit.com, so r/UGCcreators and r/TikTokShop are unchecked, not empty. I found no X, TikTok, Discord or Skool posts about Trybe outside Nate West. Most pages were read through a summarizing fetch tool, so check any number against the page before reusing it.

## 1. Other creators' experiences

- **Haley Hustles (@haley.hustles), YouTube video on Trybe (undated).** Says 20+ brands accepted her within two weeks, with products on the way. Says she earned over $30,000 a month through TikTok's creative channel before (not Trybe income) [CLAIM]. Gives illustrative math of ten $100 videos for $1,000 and a $3,000 retainer for 30 videos [CLAIM]. Says earnings are not guaranteed. Tips: apply only to brands you fit, attach sample videos, keep applying after rejections. She also promotes a Whitelist Wealth community course, so she profits from signups. https://videohighlight.com/v/_uUxKjcYBqA (third-party summary)
- **Rob Wagener, Oink for Influencers, 2026-08-26.** The profile is the application, with up to five portfolio videos. Pay is a flat fee, a share of ad spend or GMV (commonly 10 to 15 percent), or both. Some requests get accepted, but others, in his words, "Some sit there for weeks with no response." He also warns an approved video may never run as an ad. https://oinkforinfluencers.com/trybe-tutorial-2026/
- **App Store listing.** 4.9 stars from 4,300+ ratings. Four updates between Sep 2 and Sep 19 (now 1.3.1). One reviewer likes weekly payouts [CLAIM]. A creator with 4+ years in UGC says the app does some things right and some wrong. The developer reply concedes brands sometimes do not use approved videos and that the approval scoring method needs work. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/trybe-creators/id6759299465
- **Lukas Pakter (co-founder), podcast summary.** Creators earn about 90 percent of income from one or two long-term brands, using retainers plus performance pay. Best creator made $79K in a month after 11 months [CLAIM]. Advice: address the viewer as you, not I. Trybe trains newer creators. The summary also says brands should already do six figures a month before leaning on creators (wording unclear). https://videohighlight.com/v/6ei4p6f8DtM
- **zeroto1 (agency) data [CLAIM].** Its top 50 creators (0.35 percent of 14,254 active) drove 50.1 percent of $1.30M GMV. Creators who rise post a median 4 videos in month one versus 1 for others. https://zeroto1.co/insights/trybe-playbook
- **Foxwell Digital (agency, guest post by Sammy Rodrigo, Sep 11).** Allow 2 to 4 weeks from brand setup to first video. Brands prefer asking for re-films over denying. https://www.foxwelldigital.com/blog/a-creative-strategists-guide-to-trybe-sourcing-creator-videos-for-brands-and-agencies

Repeat complaints: approved but never run; requests pending for weeks; unclear approval scoring; no guarantee a brand pays (ToS 8.2, https://jointrybe.com/creator/terms-of-service).

## 3. How the profile works and how brands find you

- **Fields known from the ToS.** Legal name, email, linked social accounts. Trybe reads follower counts, engagement and audience demographics (2.2, 2.3). Age 18+, or 13+ with parental consent (2.1). Followers must be organic, or you face termination and forfeited unpaid pay (2.5). https://jointrybe.com/creator/terms-of-service Portfolio is up to five videos (Oink link above). Bio, niche tags and shipping address as profile fields: no source found, though brands approve or deny sample requests in the app (Foxwell link above), so an address is needed at that step.
- **Brand search.** A Discovery tab filters by approval rating, age, submission rate, commissions and interests. Foxwell suggests brands favor creators above 60 percent video approval. Brands spend a daily quota of 10 to 12 invites in weeks 1 and 2, and creator join requests land in an Inbox (Foxwell link). zeroto1 adds a niche filter and a sample score, which tracks whether you post after receiving product. It recruits for fit, then trains, and targets creators with some skill who have not broken out (zeroto1 link).
- **Invite-only programs.** No source found for a formal setting. Sources show brands invite from Discovery with limited credits, and creators can request to join. [INFERENCE] To get invited: keep approval and submission rates high, post fast after samples, show a clear niche.
- **What stands out [INFERENCE].** Five portfolio videos showing range inside one niche, accepting re-films, customer-first scripts.
- **Tiers.** zeroto1's VIP, Ambassador and Creator levels are that agency's program design, not confirmed Trybe-wide.
- **Partner ads and roadmap [CLAIM].** Brands may ask for partner access so ads run from your handle; zeroto1 claims 19 percent lower CPA and 13 percent higher CTR than brand-page ads. Its Sep 28, 2026 webinar notes list coming TikTok direct-to-store ads, AppLovin, Snapchat, Google, YouTube and an in-house optimizer.

## 4. Attribution and payouts

- **Method.** Trybe says server-side last-click tied to creator, submission and sale, or Meta's own reporting, with Stripe payouts in 170+ countries [CLAIM]. https://jointrybe.com Each video gets a unique ID that brands add to the landing-page URL as `?trybe=[video ID]` and/or to Meta's tracking field (Foxwell link).
- **Window.** No standard published; the ToS puts it in each program's Campaign Details (1.9). Ask before joining.
- **Rate.** Fixed once you join, no retroactive cuts (5.3). You keep earning on attributable results after leaving (10.2).
- **Clawbacks.** Only for confirmed fraud or order manipulation, refunded or fully cancelled orders, or chargebacks, and only where Campaign Details say so. They hit your next payout (5.4).
- **Timing of pay.** Flat fees are earned when the brand accepts the video (5.2.1). Commission accrues as attributed sales happen (5.2.2).
- **Schedule and minimum.** Periodic per program. Trybe may set a minimum, with smaller balances carried forward (5.9). No published figures. One reviewer says weekly [CLAIM].
- **Processor.** Third-party processors such as Stripe (5.7). The phrase Stripe Connect appears in no source I read. Trybe acts as payment facilitator (5.1).
- **Tax and countries.** W-9 (US) or W-8BEN (non-US) (5.6). No country list in the ToS; 170+ is homepage marketing.
- **Disputes.** In writing within 30 days of payout or waived (5.8). Trybe may withhold funds during disputes (9.5). If a brand is 14+ days delinquent, your license can be suspended (6.2.2). Trybe can suspend or remove content without notice (3.6).
- **Possible discrepancy.** Homepage says no minimums, but my summary of ToS 5.2.2 mentions a brand monthly minimum. Read that clause directly.
- **Top 100 ranking method and Trybe score.** No source found. Neither Foxwell nor zeroto1 mention them.

## 6. Competitors and how Trybe compares

- Trybe pitches itself as a TikTok Shop equivalent for UGC ads (https://jointrybe.com). Oink says the brand picks your video directly, no algorithm. zeroto1 describes brands inviting top TikTok Shop sellers into Trybe.
- **Hubfluence [CLAIM, competitor].** Trybe is Shopify and Meta only, with no TikTok Shop outreach or DM sequences, and costs more as brands scale. https://www.hubfluence.io/blog/trybe-review-alternative
- **Launchpoint [CLAIM, competitor].** Calls Trybe creator-side browse and opt-in; claims end-to-end campaigns, bulk ad launch, fraud detection and 125,000 verified creators. https://www.launchpointhq.com/blog/trybe-vs-launchpoint-which-is-better
- **Archive's June 2026 list [CLAIM, vendor].** Archive, UGC Buddy (Motion), GRIN, Aspire, Insense (20,000+ creators), #paid, Upfluence, Creator.co, Modash, CreatorIQ. It leaves out Trybe, Billo, Agentio and Superfiliate. https://archive.com/blog/ugc-whitelisting-tools-paid-social
- **Billo** has a creator app (listing seen, not read): https://apps.apple.com/app/id1499355971. Agentio, Superfiliate: no source found on how they compare.
- **Creator takeaway [INFERENCE].** Against flat-fee marketplaces, Trybe has uncapped upside but unpaid risk when ads never run. Against TikTok Shop, no algorithm lottery, but a brand must choose to run you, and a few creators take most of the money.
