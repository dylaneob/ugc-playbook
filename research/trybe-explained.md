### 3.2 What creators complain about
- **An approved video earns nothing until a brand actually runs it as an ad.** Trybe's own App Store reply admits brands don't always use approved videos.
- Join requests can sit for weeks with no response.
- Approval scoring is unclear.
- Sources: `sources/E-trybe-beyond-nate.md`. Reddit wasn't reachable, so it's unchecked, not empty.

### 3.3 How brands pick creators
- Brands search a **Discovery** tab filtered by approval rating, age, submission rate, commissions and interests.
- Brands are told to favor creators with **over 60% approval**.
- Invites are capped at **10 to 12 a day**.
- Brands ship samples through Shopify. A "sample score" tracks whether you post after getting product.
- First video takes 2 to 4 weeks from a brand's setup.
- Brands are told to ask for a re-film instead of rejecting.
- Sources: Foxwell Digital (VERIFIED), zeroto1.

### 3.5 The Trybe numbers that matter
zeroto1, a Trybe agency, on its own program (Oct 2026) [CLAIM]:
- Of 14,254 active creators, **the top 50 drove 50.1% of $1.30M GMV**. The top 10% drove 88.2%.
- **Creators who broke out posted a median of 4 videos in month one; the rest posted 1.**
- 74.6% of top-10% creators got there by the end of their first converting month.
- Survival by month: 50.8%, 62.2%, 85.7%, 85.4%. After a third converting month they tend to stay.

Read: it's winner-take-most. Start fast and keep volume high in month one.

### 3.6 Nate West, the top creator: what he actually does

**Numbers** (his own on-screen Trybe dashboard; self-reported but not edited text):
- About 90 days to Sep 17: 277 submissions (248 approved, 6 pending, 11 revision, 12 rejected); **92% approval; $86,318.40.** That's about $311 per submission.
- September: **$34,208.82**; best day Sep 7, $2,427.
- Claims #1 on the Trybe Top 100 (Jul 8), a "top 10 creator" (Sep 2), and #3 video with his Rythm ad (Sep 5).

**Background.** TikTok Shop affiliate since Aug 2025 (biggest video 18.7M views). Moved to Trybe in July 2026. Moved from Tampa to Boca. Uses an editor and Meta Muse to auto-upload to Trybe and TikTok. Launched **Huntr** on Oct 3 to 5, a paid Trybe brand finder with a "Tinder for brands" swipe [CLAIM].

**His content format** (how he grows): a selfie talking head with earbuds as the mic, a screenshot of the Trybe brand card, then his laptop showing the brand's site and the Meta Ad Library. A daily "Trybe brand of the day." Views mostly 1k to 9k; his Muse how-to hit 43.5k.

**His playbook, in his words** (`sources/nate-west-trybe-era-transcripts-42.md`):
1. "those five videos on your profile are really important." Big brands reject creators they haven't seen perform.
2. Get in with smaller brands first, make them money, then apply to the big ones.
3. "focus on about four or five brands at the most and put out as much volume as possible"; 30 to 50 videos per brand, then snowball.
4. New creators: volume first (100 to 300 videos), then quality. "These are meta ads. These aren't TikTok shop ads."
5. "Trybe is probably the biggest skill gap in the affiliate space." Low-quality creators don't get approved or get spend.
6. Go the extra mile: DM brands on Instagram and Facebook, and email them.
7. His brand filter:
   - $100k+/mo estimated revenue
   - ads running 60+ days in the Meta Ad Library
   - share of creator-tagged ads (low = open lane, but check they fund creator ads at all)
   - mostly video ads
   - 10%+ commission
   - an Amazon match selling 1,000+ units a month
   - a good Trybe score (how fast they review, launch and reply)
   - few creators in the program
   - earnings per creator
8. Pay:
   - Likes % of ad spend when a brand runs ads long.
   - 10%+ of GMV is good.
   - Avoid $ per order if you bundle, because bundles raise order value.
   - "do not be greedy on retainers." Ask once you've performed.
9. Copy the angle of a top-ranked Top 100 video onto your own product.

### 3.7 Inside the app (checked 2026-10-06)

**HAUS on Trybe** (brand page, VERIFIED):
- 10% of GMV (videos only), "10% Commission for 6 months"
- **$2.4M+ creator earnings across 75 creators** (about $32k each); Trybe Score 95; 4.9 stars (20 reviews)
- Top product is the "Premium Ultra-Soft Cotton Fitted" tank, so this is gohaus.com
- "Top Formats": fit creators filming selfie-style at home in fitted tees and tanks, a red stringer flex, and a "HAUS FLASH SALE 5 for 1" overlay



**Every brand page shows "Top Formats"** (its winning creator videos). That's free research on what sells for any brand.

**Pay can be hybrid.**
- Residence Supply pays $100 per submission + 12% of GMV.
- The UGC Apps invite pays $10 per submission + 15% of sales.

**Profile fields** (there is no written bio):
- Photo, full name, username, gender, birth year, country/city/state
- Niche (multi-select)
- Monthly UGC volume, current monthly UGC earnings, goal
- 5 "best videos"

Trybe's own nudge: "Upload 5 portfolio creatives to increase brand responses by 40%" [CLAIM]. Brands can filter creators by age, so the birth year field matters to them.

**Referral program:** 10% of referred creators' earnings for their first 100 days.

**Mac app quirks:**
- Opening the niche editor crashed the app (nothing saved).
- The floating tab bar ignores background taps.
- The side-menu button reads as a password field to macOS accessibility.

## 7. Guardrails

| Risk | Rule |
|---|---|
| FTC | Disclose inside the video in plain words ("ad", "sponsored"); you're responsible. |
| Trybe | Read each program's Campaign Details: attribution window, refund treatment, payout schedule. Flat-fee programs grant a perpetual license once paid. 12-month no-going-direct clause  |
| Brand fit | Never film fat burners, detox or debloat products, or any claim you'd call out. Supplements: no medical claims |
| People on camera | Signed releases for anyone in a paid ad (friends, partners, anyone else in frame) |
| Music | Organic reels often use audio you can't use in ads. Swap to licensed audio before paid use |
| Identity | Creator name everywhere public; legal name only on contracts and tax forms |
