# UGC lesson: the "but" reframe (2026-11-08)

**For UGC only (Trybe, EkkoVision, TikTok Shop ads). Not for OB's organic videos.**

## Source

- Creator: **@lcoakleyy**, series "UGC Sauce - Day 9/33".
- Creator's own claims (unverified): "coach of the #1 agency on Trybe", "a ton of converting content", "never fails". He credits the tactic to Alex Hormozi (the auto-transcript garbled the name).
- The video is a walking selfie lesson, one take with captions. The tactic is the takeaway, not the format.

## The tactic
`[A real, small negative about the product], but [a positive that outweighs it].`
- Use it once in a video, twice at most.
- His example: "I'll be honest, this jewelry is not the cheapest on the market, but it's made with quality premium materials that are built to last a lifetime. So once you buy it once, you're never going to have to buy another one again."
- Why he says it works: a little negative makes the creator believable. All positive reads as "commission breath", which destroys credibility.

## What is new vs what we already had
| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| "Not because / but because" (11-03, library #7) | Different. That one fakes a negative and reveals an offer. This one states a real negative and answers it. |
| Bot-line check, "can they feel the commission?" (Whop U3) | Same problem. This is a specific fix for it. |
| Use inside the body | **New.** A line that goes mid-script, usable in any format, not a hook shape. Added as library #40. |

## Rules before using it (OB + platform)
- The negative must be true and small: range, looks, price position, a minor hassle. Never fit, quality, safety or results.
- "Not the cheapest" has to be checked against real competitor prices first.
- The positive must be a fact from the brand's page or OB's real experience. His "built to last a lifetime, never buy another" is a promise that needs proof; don't copy it.
- On supplements the positive can't be a health claim.
- It's the brand's ad, so a negative about their product needs to survive their review. Film the same take with and without the line.

## Worked examples (true as of the 11-08 catalog; ungraded)
- **Jeans (OB's real words):** "I'll be honest, there's only blue and black denim. But these fit my legs, which is so rare."
- **Blank fitted tee:** "I'll be honest, there's nothing special to look at. No logo, no graphic. But the fit on the arms and shoulders is the whole point."
- **Creatine:** "I'll be honest, unflavored is kind of basic. But it's 3rd party tested and 200 mesh, and that's what I care about."
- **Straps:** "I'll be honest, it's one more thing in the gym bag. But my back is always stronger than my grip."
