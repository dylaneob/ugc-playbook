## New moves (added to library #2)
1. **Contrast slot:** one not-because line ends with "unlike [the rest of the category]", said from OB's own experience. This moves library #7's old-product contrast ("you won't wear [old tank]") inside a not-because line.
2. **Value restate after the price:** "which is crazy given [top benefit or proof]". The price lands next to the reason it's worth it.
3. **Proof line before the CTA:** the video only says "super popular lately", with no number. OB uses a number only where it is confirmed (220K sold, 10+ sell-outs).
4. **Variant-pick close:** the last line names the exact version to buy. For apparel: the pack, or the size reference.
