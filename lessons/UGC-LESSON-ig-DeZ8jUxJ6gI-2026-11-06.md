# UGC lesson: the three AI scripting prompts (ICP dream outcome, below the surface, deep pain)

UGC product ads only. Not OB's organic videos.

## Source
- https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeZ8jUxJ6gI/ , @ugcclubceo (Colin Reich, Trybe Coach), 50 s talking head, replying to a comment asking for his ChatGPT prompts. Watched 2026-11-06 (frames + whisper transcript).
- On-screen steps: "1. Attach a feature of the product to D.O of ICP", "2. Get below surface level", then a third step spoken only. Caption claim: scripting UGC with AI to "buy a Lamborghini" (his claim, not verified).
- Spoken, ICP = ideal customer profile = "who you want to buy it". He says he has no exact prompt to copy; he dictates to the AI by voice.

## Method in his words, then mine
Three instructions to give the AI when scripting any product video:
1. Attach a feature of the product to a dream outcome of the ICP.
2. Tell it to "get below the surface level": dream outcomes AND deep pain points for the ICP, not the obvious ones.
3. Attach features of the product to those deep pain points the ICP is struggling with.
Then dictate it in loose, conversational speech instead of a polished prompt.

Mine: it is a prompt chain that turns a spec sheet into a table of feature -> dream outcome and feature -> deep pain pairs. Each pair is a hook angle. Run pass 1 (surface), then pass 2 forces depth, then pass 3 pairs features with the deep pains.

## Rules / template
Prompt chain (paste the product facts first, from the brand's site or label only):
- P1: "Here is the product and its features. Name the ICP. For each feature, attach it to a dream outcome of that ICP."
- P2: "Now get below the surface level. Give deeper dream outcomes and deep pain points for this ICP, the ones they would not say out loud."
- P3: "Attach each feature to a deep pain point this ICP is struggling with. Write the hook for each in first person or product truth."
Guards (OB rules): hook comes from the pain or outcome, the feature is the proof beat; no "you" statements about the viewer's body; no medical or weight-loss promises; every product claim from the brand's own page; AI output is a list of angles to grade, not a finished script; invented stats stripped.

## NEW vs OB's method
- Existing: "Secret want, not the feature" (card UGC-LESSON-ig-DfFwlvYgLtl) is the human step of finding the private want. This adds the AI prompt chain that generates many pairs fast, an explicit ICP named first, and a pain-point pass (the secret-want step is mostly wants).
- Existing "not because / but because" and "Authority + promise + DQ" are script shapes. This is upstream of them: it produces the angle lines they need.
- Dictation (talk to the AI) is his delivery tip; take or leave.

## Worked examples (angle tables, then one script each)
### EkkoVision beater (95% organic cotton + 5% spandex, 350 gsm, short/regular/tall, hides gyno, #1 selling beater on TikTok, Black Friday sale)
ICP: men who lift and want a tee that makes the effort look good.
| Feature | Dream outcome (P1/P2) | Deep pain (P3) |
|---|---|---|
| 350 gsm | a shirt that hangs like a custom piece | thin shirts that cling and show every flaw |
| Hides gyno | feel confident taking the jacket off | hiding in a hoodie at the gym, private embarrassment |
| Short/regular/tall | fits the first time | tall guys whose shirts ride up, short guys drowning in fabric |
| 95% cotton + 5% spandex | moves with a lift, still holds shape | shirts that stretch out after a few washes |
Script (Black Friday, 20 s, no "you" body claims):
- Hook (product in hand): "I stopped wearing a hoodie to the gym."
- Line 2: "This beater is 350 gsm, so it hangs instead of clinging."
- Line 3: "It hides gyno. That was the whole reason I kept the hoodie on."
- Proof: pull it on, side angle, one rep. "Short, regular and tall, so the fit is right."
- Authority: "Number one selling beater on TikTok" (label as the brand's claim, show the page).
- Offer + CTA: "It's on sale for Black Friday. Link below."

### Ekko cuff strap (cuff for cable kickbacks, single-arm pushdowns, pullovers, curls; grip strap for pulldowns, rows, RDLs; widely copied, "patent coming soon"; men and women)
ICP: lifters, men and women, who train with cables and pulls.
| Feature | Dream outcome | Deep pain |
|---|---|---|
| Cuff for kickbacks, pushdowns, pullovers, curls | the target muscle does the work | the handle slipping or the hand taking over, a wasted set |
| Grip strap for pulldowns, rows, RDLs | finish the heavy set | grip failing before the back does |
| Widely copied | the original, not a knockoff | buying a copy that breaks mid-set |
| Men and women | one strap for the whole gym | gear that feels built for someone else |
Script (20 to 25 s):
- Hook: "Grip gave out before my back did. Again."
- Line 2: "Cuff version for kickbacks, pushdowns, pullovers, curls."
- Line 3: "Grip strap version for pulldowns, rows, RDLs."
- Proof: cut between a cable kickback and a row, each named on its word.
- Authority: "Widely copied, patent coming soon" (their claim).
- CTA: "Men and women both use it. Link below."
