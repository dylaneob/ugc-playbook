# UGC Club course: takeaways (2026-11-06)

Source: Colin Reich (@ugcclubceo), "UGC CLUB" on Whop (paid). This card is OB's own short summary of what he took from it, not the course material. For the full lessons, prompts and his script-writer GPT, join the course. Every money or result number is Colin's claim.

## U1. AI script writer
Paste the product page link first, then dictate a short first-person story brief (what you used before, what changed when you tried the product) instead of asking for "a script". Ask for teleprompter style, a max length, and who to target (your past self). Revise in a second message, for example asking for a bolder opening. Ask for HOOK, SCRIPT, TEXT ON SCREEN, SHOT IDEAS, CTA. Grade the output like any AI draft: strip invented stats and check every claim against the brand page.

## U2. Steal winners inside Trybe
A brand's Trybe card shows Top Formats, where the winning video's script can be copied. Feed it to the script writer asking for a similar script, a bit shorter, for your avatar. The Trybe Top 100 (phone app) is worth scrolling daily; a brand near the top where you're approved moves up your list.

## U3. Stop sounding like a bot
Three questions per line: does it sound like a bot, is it a feature or a feeling, can they feel the commission? No begging CTA; use a detached close (you're just putting them on, with or without them). Turn product facts into lived comparisons. Use your own slang. Only say "sells out" if it has.

## U4. Structure of his best ad (his claim)
He shows roughly a 47% hook rate, 0.9% link CTR and 44% average watch on a 48 s ad as his reference bar. Beat order: a trend green-screen hook while the trend is hot, then show the problem on camera (problem, dream outcome, problem, solution; never outcome straight to solution), the solution, the product used several ways, offer plus size reference, detached close. Jokes throughout.

## U5. Filming a script
Script in a doc, double-spaced, one line per row. Read it all once out loud. Film one line, pause about 10 seconds, film the next; chop afterwards.

## U6. Trybe profile
Link Instagram and TikTok in Settings, Social accounts (brands can run ads through your handle). Profile photo shows your unfair advantage. Fill portfolio slots fast by pasting TikTok Shop ad links (OB's rule: ads only, no organic). Check the New Brands tab; small creator counts are the openings.

## U7. Submission floor (his claims)
No verdict on a brand or the method before about 300 submissions. Pace 3 to 5 videos a day. 100 to 200 videos for a brand that never spends is reps; move on.
