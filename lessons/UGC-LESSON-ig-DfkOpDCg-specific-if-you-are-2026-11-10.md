# UGC lesson: the specific "If you are" hook (Colin Reich, 2026-11-10)

Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfkOpDCg-_v/ (@ugcclubceo, Colin Reich, 1:38). Transcribed locally (whisper large-v3-turbo); on-screen cards checked at 0:38 and 1:10.
His claims only: $54K in the last 30 days, a video with this hook on the Trybe Top 100, "it will work forever".
Builds on library format #4 (C1, from DfOHHu2tEms). New here: the qualifier stack and where to find the qualifiers.

## The formula
**"If you are [specific avatar] + [specific pain point OR dream outcome]"**, then the product.
The skill is the specificity. Add qualifiers until the right viewer feels named:
- gender, age, occupation, lifestyle, habits (drinks several times a week, sober, trains X days, and so on)
- His ladder: "If you're a mom" → "a busy mom in your 30s" → "on a GLP-1" → "who's struggling to hit her protein goals".

## Where the qualifiers come from (his 5 steps, on screen at 1:10)
1. Customer reviews.
2. Comments on viral videos (search the pain point or dream outcome on TikTok, read the comments).
3. Who the brand targets in its ads (Meta Ad Library long-runners) and the site's copy.
4. The buyer's biggest pain points and dream outcomes: ask what the product solves, then what that does for them below the surface level (same move as the secret-want step).
5. An AI pass to make it more specific (grade the output; facts from the brand's page only).

## OB's rules on top
- Qualifiers describe life, habits and situations, never the viewer's body ("you" body claims stay banned). The pain sits on the product problem.
- One variation per product per batch, like every other format.
- The avatar has to be someone OB can credibly speak to (lifters, guys 30+, busy schedules).

## Ladders for OB's live products (drafts, ungraded)
**Ekko Lifestyle Beater**
1. "If you're a guy who lifts..."
2. "...in your 30s who lifts four days a week..."
3. "...and every tank top you own either shows your whole chest or fits like a dress..."
→ "this is the one I've worn for two years." (true for OB: 2+ years, hundreds of washes)
Alt dream-outcome close: "...and you want one shirt that works at the gym and at dinner after."
Alt avatar: "If you're a dad who still trains at 5 a.m. before the kids are up and your gym shirts are all stretched out..."

**Ekko straight-leg jeans**
"If you're a guy who lifts, wears jeans out after the gym, and every slim fit feels like leggings by the time you sit down..."
Alt: "If you're 30-plus, done with skinny jeans, and don't want to look like your dad either..."

**Fish oil / magnesium / multivitamin** (no medical or weight-loss promises; label facts only)
Fish oil: "If you're a guy who trains hard, eats fish maybe once a month, and takes whatever's cheapest on Amazon..."
Magnesium: "If you're a lifter who's up at 5, on your third coffee by noon, and never thinks about magnesium..."
Multivitamin: "If you're a busy guy in your 30s who eats the same five meals every week..."

## Variation axes
Avatar (age, job, schedule, habit) × pain vs dream outcome × close line. Film 3 to 4 avatar openers on one body; the cut is the hook swap.
