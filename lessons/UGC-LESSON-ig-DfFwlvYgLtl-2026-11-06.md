# UGC angle lesson: sell what people want secretly, not the feature (2026-11-06)

**For UGC only (Trybe, EkkoVision, TikTok Shop ads). Not for OB's organic videos.**

## The method in my words
1. A feature never sells the product. His line: "No one cares what the t-shirt is made of." They care what it does for them.
2. Find what the buyer wants secretly: the private motive or private pain they would not say out loud. His example: wanting to out-earn a dad, brother or husband, or fund a trip. Wanting is the dream outcome; the pain point is its mirror.
3. Attach the product to that dream outcome or pain point, below the surface level. Surface = "soft fabric". Below the surface = the private worry the fabric solves.
4. Hitting it is what stops the scroll. His examples: leaky gut and white underwear; thinning hair on a GLP.
5. His rank: this is his number one piece of advice on Trybe, above any format or hook shape.

## Rules (UGC)
- Before writing any script: list the product's features, then for each ask "what private worry or private win does this solve?" Write the hook from the private one.
- The feature stays in the script as proof, after the hook, never as the hook.
- Secret motive must be about a purchase or a product truth, said in first person or as the product's job. No "you" claims about the viewer's body.
- OB's compliance still holds: no medical or weight-loss promises on supplements, no invented stats, every spec from the brand's own page.

## NEW vs OB's method
OB's skill already has: hook in 9 words or 3 s, one reason to buy, hook as the unit of variation, the 11-03 "not because / but because" shape (which already bans bare features) and the 11-06 authority + DQ hook.
**New:** the upstream step. The two existing shapes tell you how to arrange lines; this says how to pick the raw material: the secret motive, found before the hook exists. It also adds a discovery list (feature, surface benefit, secret motive) as a per-product worksheet and a test: if the hook line could appear on the product's spec sheet, rewrite it.

## WORKED EXAMPLE 1: EkkoVision beater (Black Friday sale)
Facts: 95% organic cotton + 5% spandex, 350 gsm, short/regular/tall, hides gyno, #1 selling beater on TikTok, Black Friday sale.
| Feature | Surface benefit | Secret want / pain |
|---|---|---|
| 350 gsm thick cotton | feels sturdy | a shirt that does not cling or show the chest shape through the fabric |
| short/regular/tall | fits properly | stops pulling it down all day |
| hides gyno (brand claim) | looks good | being able to take the shirt off in the gym photo or the mirror without a thought |

> **HOOK (product on screen, first person):** "I stopped thinking about my chest at the gym."
> **REASON:** "It's 350 gsm, 95% organic cotton and 5% spandex, so nothing shows through. Short, regular and tall."
> **PROOF BEAT:** fabric held up to light, stretch test, three lengths side by side; TikTok Shop rank screenshot for the #1 line.
> **OFFER + CTA:** "It's the number one selling beater on TikTok, and it's on Black Friday sale now. Link's below."

Notes: OB must confirm the first-person line is true for him before filming. "Hides gyno" stays the brand's claim, shown as the product's job. Confirm sale end date.

## WORKED EXAMPLE 2: Ekko cuff strap (no sale)
Facts: cuff for cable kickbacks, single-arm pushdowns, pullovers, curls; grip strap for pulldowns, rows, RDLs; widely copied, "patent coming soon"; men and women.
| Feature | Surface benefit | Secret want / pain |
|---|---|---|
| cuff on the wrist or ankle | better cable angles | not having your grip give out before the target muscle does |
| grip strap for pulls | stronger hold | finishing the set because the muscle quit, not the hands |
| widely copied | popular | wanting the original, not the knockoff everyone else bought |

> **HOOK:** "My hands used to quit before my glutes did."
> **REASON:** "The cuff takes cable kickbacks, single-arm pushdowns, pullovers and curls. The grip strap takes pulldowns, rows and RDLs. Men and women both use it."
> **PROOF BEAT:** kickback on the cuff, then a pulldown on the grip strap. "It's been widely copied, patent coming soon."
> **CTA:** "Get the original. Link's below."

Notes: "patent coming soon" only until the owner confirms filing status. No competitor brands on screen. First-person lines need OB's confirmation.

## Variation sheet
- Hook source: swap the secret motive (self-image, hands quitting, the knockoff, wearing it in photos).
- Same take, new first line, feature stays as proof.
- Pair with the existing shapes: "not because" lines can be built from this worksheet.
