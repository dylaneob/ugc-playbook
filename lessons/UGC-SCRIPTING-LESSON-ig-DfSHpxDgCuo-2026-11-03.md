**For UGC only (Trybe, EkkoVision, TikTok Shop ads). Not for OB's organic videos.**

## The method in my words
1. The hook is a fake-negative opener: stay away from / I'm returning [product]. It breaks the pattern of a review ad and forces the "why?" question.
2. Then comes "not because" plus a feature tied to a dream outcome or a pain point. Never the bare feature. Say it 2 or 3 times (he calls it "X2 or 3"), each a different benefit.
3. The reversal: "but because" plus the real reason, which is a new offer, a new sale, or a funnier dream-outcome twist. Then introduce the product and "make it make sense".
4. The mistake he warns about: listing features alone. Features must carry a dream outcome (what life looks like) or a pain point (what it ends).
5. Two reveal types. Offer reveal: the reason is the sale (Black Friday, BOGO, price drop, cheaper than a gym membership). No-offer reveal: the reason is an over-the-top consequence ("you're going to throw away all your hats", "empty your closet"), a joke.
6. Three worked examples in the reel: whitening strips, walking pad, hair growth serum. Each is one breath, about 15 to 25 seconds spoken. Tone is casual and slightly tongue in cheek.
7. Variation logic: swap product, swap the three "not because" lines, swap the reveal (offer vs joke). Final tip: feed the reel to an AI and have it write scripts for your product.

Short quotes (his): "stay the hell away from this product"; "attach a dream outcome to the feature"; "make it make sense".

## SCRIPT TEMPLATE (fill in the blanks)
1. **HOOK (0 to 3 s, product on screen):** "I'm returning [product]." or "Stay away from [product]."
2. **NOT BECAUSE x3 (each one line, feature + outcome, 3 to 5 s each):**
   - "Not because [feature] means [dream outcome]."
   - "Not because [feature] ends [pain point]."
   - "And definitely not because [feature] lets [first-person result]."
3. **BUT BECAUSE (the reveal, 3 to 5 s):** "But because [sale / price / deal]." OR no-offer version: "But because [absurd consequence, a joke]."
4. **PROOF BEAT (3 to 5 s, optional):** show the product doing the feature, demo or fit check, matching the words.
5. **CTA (2 to 4 s):** "Link is below, [deal end date]."
Length 15 to 30 s. Variations: 8 to 12 alternate first lines and "not because" lines, 2 reveals (offer, joke).

**OB rules layered on top:** feature lines say product truth or "I" statements, never "you" claims about the viewer's body. "Dream outcome" is written as what I feel or what the product does. No invented stats. "Patent coming soon", never "patented". Hook still has to land in 9 words or 3 s.

## NEW vs OB's method
OB's method (ugc skill section 4/5) already has: hook in 9 words or 3 s, product by 2 s, one reason to buy, offer, CTA, hook as the unit of variation, 8 to 12 hooks per script.
**New from this reel:**
- A concrete hook shape: fake-negative opener plus "not because / but because".
- The rule that a feature never stands alone; every feature is paired with a dream outcome or pain point.
- The "x3 then flip" rhythm: three outcome lines, then one reveal.
- The reveal choice: offer reveal (sale) vs joke reveal (no sale). This gives OB a clean variation axis separate from first-frame changes.
- Using the sale itself as the payoff line (fits Black Friday).
- Caution: the pure "I'm returning it" opener risks reading as a negative review. Needs the reveal fast and the on-screen product to make the joke clear.

## WORKED EXAMPLE 1: EkkoVision beater (Black Friday sale)
Facts used: 95% organic cotton + 5% spandex, 350 gsm, short/regular/tall, hides gyno, #1 selling beater on TikTok, Black Friday sale.

> **HOOK:** "I'm returning this beater."
> Not because it's 350 gsm and nothing shows through it.
> Not because it comes in short, regular and tall, so it actually fits.
> And definitely not because it's 95% organic cotton and 5% spandex, so I wear it under everything and stop thinking about my chest.
> But because it's the number one selling beater on TikTok, and it's on Black Friday sale right now.
> *(proof beat: shirt over it, then stretch test on camera)*
> **CTA:** "Link's below, sale ends when Black Friday does."

Notes: "hides gyno" appears only as first-person ("stop thinking about my chest"), no claim about the viewer. OB must confirm any personal line is true. Confirm which SKUs are 350 gsm (see flag in EKKO-BUYER-DESIRES file). Confirm sale end date before saying it.

## WORKED EXAMPLE 2: Ekko cuff strap (no sale, joke reveal)
Facts used: cuff for cable kickbacks, single-arm pushdowns, pullovers, curls; grip strap for pulldowns, rows, RDLs; widely copied, patent coming; men and women.

> **HOOK:** "Stay away from this cuff."
> Not because it makes cable kickbacks and single-arm pushdowns finally feel right.
> Not because the grip strap takes grip out of the picture on pulldowns, rows and RDLs.
> And definitely not because pullovers and curls both work with the same cuff, for men and for women.
> But because every brand copied it, patent coming soon, and now my gym looks like a cuff convention.
> *(proof beat: kickback, then a pulldown with the grip strap)*
> **CTA:** "Get the original, link below."

Notes: "widely copied" is safe; "patent coming soon" only until the owner confirms filing status. Joke reveal used because there is no sale; swap to the Black Friday offer line when running promo.

## Variation sheet (for the film sheet)
- Openers: "I'm returning", "Stay away from", "Do not buy", "I regret buying" (the last is a hook variant, must still resolve by the reveal).
- Reveals: sale, price vs gym membership/competitor, joke consequence, "number one seller" fact.
- Same take, swap the first line and first frame (text hook line, product in hand).
