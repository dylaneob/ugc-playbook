Source: UGC Club, "The 4 Hooks I Use to Make $50K/Month on Trybe", 7:51, https://youtu.be/xlZuQhsaCo8 (same video as IG reel DfhzpqyAlUr, sent by OB 11-09). Read: full transcript and 3 frames. It is a screen recording of a Google Doc with his face in a circle.

His claims only, none checked: $52K in the last 30 days on Trybe, a video at #59 on the Trybe Top 100 using hook 3, "these four hooks always work", "any product ever", and "Meta listens" (that saying a specific audience's words makes Meta target them). ## The four frameworks, his words

1. **Specific person + dream outcome + pain point.** `If you're [specific person] trying to [dream outcome] without [pain point], [introduce product].` Already library #4. His correction: "if you're a guy" fails; the person and the pain have to be below the surface.
2. **Stay the hell away.** `If you're [specific person] struggling with [specific pain point], stay the hell away from [product]. Not because of [feature + dream outcome]. Not because of [feature + dream outcome]. And definitely not because of [feature + dream outcome]. But because [logical consequence, dream outcome, convenience or relatable pain point].` His ending: "you might end up throwing out every other shirt in your closet."
3. **I'm returning.** `I'm returning [product]. Not because x3. But because I paid full price when I could have got it for [amount].` "Build the desire first and reveal the offer second." Don't mention the sale until the last line. He says to read the brand's creative brief and use its offer.
4. **The specific pain point stack.** `If you're [specific person] and you've been [pain 1], [pain 2], and [pain 3], [introduce product].` Three fast, specific pains. His reason: the viewer feels understood before the product appears.

## What is actually new

- **Library #45:** person callout in front of Stay Away and Not Because. #2 and #7 existed separately.
- **Library #46:** the three-pain stack.
- **The test for a pain:** "They don't care about their acne. They care about what the acne does to their life." Write what the problem makes them do: checking a mirror mid-date, avoiding pictures.
- **Correction to our library:** #2's beater example was bare features ("Not because it's 350 gsm"), the exact mistake he calls out. Fixed.
- **Four questions per product:** who is this for, why do they want it, what problem does it solve, what do they secretly wish would happen. Same ladder as the De9wAgVA3XB step, with "who" added on top.
- **Jokes are allowed on the outcome lines:** "so your gym crush looks at you".

## Limits for OB's ads

- **Don't copy his examples.** "Little dad belly", acne, low testosterone, "busy mom on a GLP" and "feel 22 again" are viewer-body or health claims. OB's pains stay on the garment, the buying habit or the situation.
- Every "if you've been" line must be something buyers really do. Prefer public buyer language (`EKKO-BUYER-LANGUAGE-2026-11-09.md`).
- Stack hooks run 30 words or more, well past the 9-word target. Like #43, one variation per product per batch.
- "I'm returning" reads as a bad review, so reveal fast. The sale reveal waits for Ekko's written yes. "I paid full price" has to be true, or use a joke reveal.
- "Stay the hell away" needs a clean take with "stay away".
- No competitor names, invented stats or scarcity in the "but because" line.
