## Rules before using these (OB + platform)
- **Hook 01 on Meta:** "in your 40s" states the viewer's age, and Meta's personal-attributes rule covers age. These ads run on Meta. Use a first-person or third-person version: "As a dad in my 40s..." or "For dads in their 40s...". No "you" lines about the viewer's body (skill rule).
- **Hook 02:** "the hell" may fail a brand's guide. Check the guide, otherwise use "Stay away from". The offer must be the real, current one. Reveal fast or it reads as a bad review.
- **Hook 03:** the authority has to be real and shown on screen. No proof, no hook. Facts come from the brand's page.

## Worked examples (fill offers from the brand on the day)
**EkkoVision beater**
- 01: "If you're the guy who wants a beater that stays put without pulling it down all day, it comes in short, regular and tall."
- 02: "Stay away from this beater. Not because it's 350 gsm and nothing shows through. Not because it comes in short, regular and tall. But because [real Black Friday offer]."
- 03: "This is the number one selling beater on TikTok. It's the one tank that doesn't cling, and it's because of the 350 gsm cotton." (Rank on screen, brand claim.)
